# Why Sign In with a Password

We have found that some accounts may encounter device code flow authorization issues when attempting to sign in by non-password methods, and thus cannot sign in successfully. After we recommended that these users switch to password sign-in, their sign-in success rate improved significantly.

# How to Sign In to a Microsoft Account with a Password

- When signing in, check whether the sign-in page has an "Other ways to sign in" option. As soon as you see this option, click it immediately.

![Click the "Other ways to sign in" option](/assets/img/docs/use-password-login-microsoft-account/1.png)

- On the sign-in page, the system will ask which sign-in method you want to use. Select "Use my password", then enter your password to complete the sign-in.

_If there is no "Use my password" option, or if you have forgotten your password, see the [tutorial below](#add-or-change-a-password)._

![Select "Use my password"](/assets/img/docs/use-password-login-microsoft-account/2.png)

# Add or Change a Password

Some accounts may not have a password set up for sign-in, or you may have forgotten the password. Please follow the steps below:

- Go to the [Add/Change Password](https://account.live.com/password/Change) page;

- Follow the instructions on that page to add a password. If you have forgotten your password, you can choose the "Forgot password?" option on that page to recover it.

![Add a password](/assets/img/docs/use-password-login-microsoft-account/3.png)

![Change the password](/assets/img/docs/use-password-login-microsoft-account/4.png)

After completing the steps above, follow the [tutorial above](#how-to-sign-in-to-a-microsoft-account-with-a-password) to sign in to your Microsoft account with a password.
